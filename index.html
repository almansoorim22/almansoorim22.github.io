Faunotion Uploader

Faunotion Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the Faunotion YouTube channel. It uploads finished videos that the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API, sets their title, description and disclosure settings, and checks their processing status.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users, and it only acts on YouTube channels that the owner has personally authorised.
